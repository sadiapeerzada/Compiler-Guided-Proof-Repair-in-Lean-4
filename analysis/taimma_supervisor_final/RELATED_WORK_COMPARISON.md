# Related-work comparison and narrow contribution

Primary papers checked on 4 November 2026. This is an evidence comparison for author review, not an exhaustive systematic review or manuscript/bibliography edit. “Not verified” means the inspected source does not establish the requested field; it does not prove a method lacks that control. Rates across different benchmarks and budgets are not rankings.

Li and Li directly study matched diagnostics, failed predecessors, copying and equal-token retrying. Those ideas cannot be claimed as first introduced here. Baldur already establishes whole-proof generation with compiler-guided repair; APRIL establishes supervised repair from compiler diagnostics. LeanDojo contributes retrieval/tooling/split design rather than the same intervention. COPRA adds stateful agent search; its workflow gains do not isolate diagnostic content. These distinctions follow the primary sources linked in the rows.

A defensible contribution statement is: “We report a small, auditable investigation of fixed-statement proof repair with locally executed quantized 7B general and coding models in Lean 4. We separate an original low-success pilot from a constructed multi-arm cohort, expose blind/shuffled/token-matched/resampling comparisons, and provide paired theorem/family-aware uncertainty and resource accounting. Results illustrate the limits of diagnostic-specific benefit in this setting rather than establish a new repair algorithm or a general superiority claim.” This describes the repository's combined evidence package; global uniqueness of that combination has not been established.

No benchmark-independence, contamination-free, causal-success, state-of-the-art, or faithful NL-to-Lean claim is supported. The constructed cohort has provisional mathematical overlap and placeholder English. Study 1 and Study 2 have different tasks/protocol histories; V8 is not a confirmatory replication and V9 cannot supply independent held-out evidence.

Bibliographic discrepancy: the inspected [APRIL v1 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.02990v1) and [v2 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.02990v2) name **Yiran Wang**, while the [official abstract metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02990) names **Evan Wang**.
