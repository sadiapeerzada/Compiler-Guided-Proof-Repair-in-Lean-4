# Frozen dataset attribution and evidence

This is the versioned AI-assisted study dataset. Original records and human-review fields are preserved; their historical status is not converted into a human approval.

The 16 textbook-derived statement adaptations retain Oscar Levin's *Discrete Mathematics: An Open Introduction*, third edition, item-level citations and CC BY-SA 4.0 attribution. Those adapted textbook statements are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0, with changes described in each source record. Archived publisher pages, colophon and PDF bytes are hash-bound in each eligible record.

The 59 formal-library-derived statements retain the exact pinned Mathlib/Lean repository, item location, Apache-2.0 notice and archived original copyright headers. Their English statements are author-assisted paraphrases/adaptations of formal source material, not falsely attributed English quotations. Source files and licenses remain in their original versioned archives. Each item's original license and attribution remain applicable; this notice does not relicense the whole repository or model weights.

Source/rights assessments are AI-assisted interpretations of the cited published license evidence, not invented publisher permission, legal clearance, human review, or publisher endorsement. The original 20 nonduplicate statements without adequate item provenance are excluded despite their proofs; the exact duplicate is separately excluded. Both corrected original examples retain their correction/review history and remain excluded for provenance.

Fifteen development and 60 test statements are separated by curated theorem families. There are five development-family clusters and 54 test-family clusters; these counts are disclosed rather than claiming 75 independent topic families. All source/dataset/family selections precede primary-model held-out proof generation. Family-cluster statistical sensitivity and limitations are pre-specified in the final protocol.
