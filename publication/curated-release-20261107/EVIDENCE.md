**Why the full archive is not copied:** The indexed model-interaction journal contains additional material requiring a documented privacy and licensing review. Cloning the complete source repository is the route to archival inspection. A small clean presentation package cannot be substituted for a full reproducibility deposit.

**Scientific boundaries:** The original 60-type study and constructed 24-type three-seed study are different. V8 is exploratory; V9 is development-only. A successful analysis reproduction, checksum match, formal relation certificate, and rerun of model inference are **distinct** verification claims. Independent semantic adjudication for the benchmark is not established.
