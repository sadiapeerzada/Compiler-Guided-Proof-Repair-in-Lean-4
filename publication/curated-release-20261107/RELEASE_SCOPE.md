# Release scope and integrity statement

**Source:** https://github.com/sadiapeerzada/Compiler-Guided-Proof-Repair-in-Lean-4/commit/adf16acee82cad915a2012c1a7856cbbadc20a13

This package is a presentation-oriented companion to a two-study empirical paper. Its numerical CSVs are unmodified source-file copies with SHA-256 values recorded in `manifest.json`. It does not include complete model-interaction journals, model weights, all source snapshots, a runnable inference environment or original manuscript assets. It therefore does **not** claim complete reconstruction of the experiments.

## Matters readers must know

1. The outcome measures are strictly verified **proofs for fixed formal statements**, not independently validated English-to-Lean translations.
2. Study 1 has **two unavailable compiler-guided endpoints** and one shared solved theorem across B and C on 58 complete pairs.
3. Study 2 reuses 24 constructed theorem types across three seeds. Family relations and identified formal overlaps limit claims about effective independence.
4. Matched versus shuffled diagnostics are insufficient for claiming a diagnostic-content-specific causal effect; clustered intervals span zero.
5. The Coder V8 run is exploratory; V9 is development-only. Neither establishes a confirmatory held-out replication.
6. The source audits document unresolved independent semantic/mathematical adjudication and privacy/licensing questions. They are **not represented as resolved** by this curated release.
7. The public original archive retains source-specific third-party attribution and usage constraints; this package grants **no blanket license** to those materials.

The file selection was intentionally narrow to avoid accidental republishing of local identifiers or unnecessary historical development material. It must still be checked by the responsible researchers before public release.
