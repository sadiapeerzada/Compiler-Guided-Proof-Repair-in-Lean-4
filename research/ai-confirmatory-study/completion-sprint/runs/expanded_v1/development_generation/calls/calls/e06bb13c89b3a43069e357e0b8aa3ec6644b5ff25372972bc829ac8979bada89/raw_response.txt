STATEMENT: For any non-empty finite interval [a, b] of natural numbers and any function f : ℕ → M where M is a commutative monoid, the product of f over the interval [a, b] is equal to the product of f over the interval [a + 1, b + 1] multiplied by f(a).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem sprint_development_0094 {M : Type*} [CommMonoid M] {a b : ℕ} (hab : a ≤ b) (f : ℕ → M) :
  ∏ k ∈ Finset.Ico a (b + 1), f k = (∏ k ∈ Finset.Ico a b, f k) * f b :=

  rw [Finset.prod_Ico_succ_top hab]
```
