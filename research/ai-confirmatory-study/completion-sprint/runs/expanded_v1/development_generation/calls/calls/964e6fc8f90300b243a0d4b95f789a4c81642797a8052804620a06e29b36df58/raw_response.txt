STATEMENT: For any sets \( A \) and \( B \), if \( A \) is a subset of \( B \), then the Cartesian product \( A \times A \) is a subset of the Cartesian product \( B \times B \).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem sprint_development_0011 {A B : Set α} (hAB : A ⊆ B) : A × A ⊆ B × B :=
  by
  intros x y
  have hxA : x ∈ A := hAB x
  have hyA : y ∈ A := hAB y
  have hxB : x ∈ B := hxA
  have hyB : y ∈ B := hyA
  exact prod.intro hxB hyB
```
