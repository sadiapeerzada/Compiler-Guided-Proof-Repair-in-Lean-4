STATEMENT: For any finite set `s` and any element `a` not in `s`, the cardinality of `s` is equal to the cardinality of `s` union `{a}`.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem sprint_development_0071 {α : Type u_1} {s : Finset α} {a : α} (h : a ∉ s) : s.card = (s ∪ {a}).card := by
  rw [Finset.card_union, Finset.card_insert_of_not_mem h]
```
