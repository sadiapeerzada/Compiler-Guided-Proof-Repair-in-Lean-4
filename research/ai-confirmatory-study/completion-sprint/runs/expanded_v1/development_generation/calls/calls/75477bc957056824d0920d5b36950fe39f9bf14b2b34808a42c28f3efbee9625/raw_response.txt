STATEMENT: For any finite set \( s \) and any element \( a \) not in \( s \), the cardinality of the set \( s \) with \( a \) added is equal to the cardinality of \( s \) plus one.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem sprint_development_0103 {α : Type*} {s : Finset α} {a : α} (ha : a ∉ s) : (s ∪ {a}).card = s.card + 1 := by
  rw [Finset.card_union_of_disjoint, Finset.card_insert_of_not_mem ha]
```
