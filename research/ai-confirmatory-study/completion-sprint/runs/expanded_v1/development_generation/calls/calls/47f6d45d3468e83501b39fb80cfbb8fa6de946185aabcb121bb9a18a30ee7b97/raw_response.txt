STATEMENT: For any injective function \( f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N} \) and any finite set \( s : \text{Finset} \mathbb{N} \), the image of \( s \) under \( f \) is also a finite set.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem sprint_development_0120 (f : ℕ → ℕ) (s : Finset ℕ) (hf : Function.Injective f) : Finset.Finite (Finset.map f s) :=
  by
    rw [Finset.map_val]
    apply Multiset.Finite.map hf
```
