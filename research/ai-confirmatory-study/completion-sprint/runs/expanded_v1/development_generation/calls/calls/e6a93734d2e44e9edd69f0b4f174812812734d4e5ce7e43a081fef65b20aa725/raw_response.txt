STATEMENT: For any injective function \( f \) and any finite set \( s \), the image of \( s \) under \( f \) is a subset of the image of \( s \) under \( f \).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem sprint_development_0008 {α : Type*} {β : Type*} (f : α → β) (s : Finset α) (hf : Function.Injective f) : Finset.image f s ⊆ Finset.image f s :=
  by
  intros b hb
  apply Finset.mem_image
  use b
  exact hb
```
