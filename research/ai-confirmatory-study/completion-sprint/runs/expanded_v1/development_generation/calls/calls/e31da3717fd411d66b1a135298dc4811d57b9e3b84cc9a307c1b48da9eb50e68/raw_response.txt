STATEMENT: For any finite interval [a, b] of natural numbers and any function f : ℕ → M where M is a commutative monoid, the product of f over [a, b] is equal to the product of f over [a + c, b + c] shifted by c.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem sprint_development_0014 {M : Type*} [CommMonoid M] {a b c : ℕ} (hab : a ≤ b) (f : ℕ → M) :
  ∏ x ∈ Finset.Ico a b, f x = ∏ x ∈ Finset.Ico (a + c) (b + c), f (x - c) :=
by
  rw [Finset.prod_Ico_add_right_sub_eq]
  exact hab
```
