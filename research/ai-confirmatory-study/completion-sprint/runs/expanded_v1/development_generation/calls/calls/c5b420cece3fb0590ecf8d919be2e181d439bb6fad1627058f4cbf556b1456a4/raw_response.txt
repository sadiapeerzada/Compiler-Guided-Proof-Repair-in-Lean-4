STATEMENT: For any finite set `s` and any element `a` not in `s`, the cardinality of the set `s` union `{a}` is equal to the cardinality of `s` plus one.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem sprint_development_0023 {α : Type u_1} {s : Finset α} {a : α} (h : a ∉ s) : s.card + 1 = (s ∪ {a}).card := by
  rw [Finset.card_union, Finset.card_insert_of_not_mem h]
```
