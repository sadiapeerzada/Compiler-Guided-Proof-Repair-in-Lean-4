STATEMENT: If two finite sets \( s \) and \( t \) are disjoint, then their union is the disjoint union of \( s \) and \( t \).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem sprint_development_0121 {α : Type} {s t : Finset α} (h : s ⊓ t = ∅) : s ∪ t = s.disjiUnion t h :=
  by
  rw [Finset.union_eq_disjiUnion]
  exact h
```
