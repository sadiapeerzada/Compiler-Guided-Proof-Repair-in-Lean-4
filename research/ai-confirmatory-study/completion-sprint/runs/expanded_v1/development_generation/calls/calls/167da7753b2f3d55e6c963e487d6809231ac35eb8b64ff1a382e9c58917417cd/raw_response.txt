STATEMENT: For any finite set `s` and any element `a` not in `s`, the cardinality of `s` is equal to the cardinality of `s` union `{a}`.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem sprint_development_0055 {α : Type*} {s : Finset α} {a : α} (ha : a ∉ s) : s.card = (s ∪ {a}).card :=
by
  rw [Finset.card_union, Finset.card_insert_of_not_mem ha]
```
