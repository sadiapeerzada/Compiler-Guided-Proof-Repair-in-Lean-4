STATEMENT: If two sets \( s \) and \( t \) are both subsets of their respective sets \( s' \) and \( t' \), then their Cartesian products \( s \times t \) and \( s' \times t' \) are also subsets of each other.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem sprint_development_0107 {α : Type} {β : Type} {s : Set α} {t : Set β} {s' : Set α} {t' : Set β} (hs : s ⊆ s') (ht : t ⊆ t') : s × t ⊆ s' × t' := by
  rintro (a, b)
  exact prod_mono hs ht a b
```
