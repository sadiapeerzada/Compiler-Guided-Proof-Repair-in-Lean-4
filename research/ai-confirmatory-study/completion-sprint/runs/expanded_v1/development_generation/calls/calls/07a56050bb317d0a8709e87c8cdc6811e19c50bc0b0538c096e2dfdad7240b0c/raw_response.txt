STATEMENT: For any lists `l₁` and `l₂` of equal length, the projection of the zip of `l₁` and `l₂` onto the first component is equal to `l₁`.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem sprint_development_0099 {α : Type} {β : Type} (l₁ : List α) (l₂ : List β) (h : l₁.length = l₂.length) :
  List.map Prod.fst (l₁.zip l₂) = l₁ :=
  by
    rw [List.zipWith_zipWith_left (fun _ _ => Prod.fst) (fun x => x) l₁ l₂ (List.map Prod.fst l₂) h]
    simp
```
