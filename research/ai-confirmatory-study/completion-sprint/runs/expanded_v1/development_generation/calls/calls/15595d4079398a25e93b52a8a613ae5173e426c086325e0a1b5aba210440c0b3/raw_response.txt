STATEMENT: For any injective function \( f : \mathbb{N} \to \mathbb{N} \) and any finite set \( s \subseteq \mathbb{N} \), the image of \( s \) under \( f \) is also a finite set.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem sprint_development_0056 {f : ℕ → ℕ} (hf : Function.Injective f) {s : Finset ℕ} : Finset.isFinite (Finset.map f s) := by
  rw [Finset.map_val]
  apply Multiset.isFinite_map hf
```
