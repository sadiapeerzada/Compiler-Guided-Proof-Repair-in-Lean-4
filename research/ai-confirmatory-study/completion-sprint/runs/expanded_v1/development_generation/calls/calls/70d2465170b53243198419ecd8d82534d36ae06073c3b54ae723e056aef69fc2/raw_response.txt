STATEMENT: For any finite set \( s \) and any element \( a \) not in \( s \), the cardinality of \( s \) is equal to the cardinality of \( s \) with \( a \) added.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem sprint_development_0007 {α : Type*} {s : Finset α} {a : α} (h : a ∉ s) : s.card = (s.insert a).card :=
  by
  rw [Finset.card_insert_of_not_mem h]
```
