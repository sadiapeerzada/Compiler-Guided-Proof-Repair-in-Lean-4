# Contamination scope

The inherited 193-version inventory and exposure registry supply deterministic
exact, normalized/template and family screens for formally verified candidates.
Each reviewer receives five lexical historical neighbors, without another review
or the reference proof. These methods do not certify absence of mathematical
equivalence, specialization, proof-template overlap, or training contamination.
Unassessed model-pretraining contamination remains separate from detected exposure.
No Lean implication certificates were generated in this pilot. All proposals are
excluded from future untouched test use, including rejected or inspected proposals.
See reviews/historical_overlap_screen.csv for actual per-candidate screens.
