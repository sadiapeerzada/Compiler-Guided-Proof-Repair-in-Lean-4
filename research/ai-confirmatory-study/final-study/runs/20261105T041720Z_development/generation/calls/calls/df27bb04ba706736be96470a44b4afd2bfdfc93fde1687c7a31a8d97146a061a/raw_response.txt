STATEMENT: For any finite sets \(A\) and \(B\), and any function \(f: A \times B \to \mathbb{Z}\), the sum of the sums of \(f\) over all elements of \(A\) is equal to the sum of the sums of \(f\) over all elements of \(B\), when reindexed appropriately.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0068 {A B : Set ℕ} {f : A × B → ℤ} : 
  (∑ a in A, ∑ b in B, f (a, b)) = (∑ b in B, ∑ a in A, f (a, b)) := by
  have h : ∀ a b, f (a, b) = f (b, a) := by
    intro a b
    simp [Function.update]
  rw [h]
  congr
  apply Finset.sum_comm
```
