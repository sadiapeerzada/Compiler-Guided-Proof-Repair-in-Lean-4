STATEMENT: For any positive integer \( n \) and any real number \( a \), the sum of the first \( n \) terms of a geometric series with the first term \( a \) and common ratio \( a \) is given by \( \frac{a(1 - a^n)}{1 - a} \) if \( a \neq 1 \).

  · simp [Finset.range_zero, a_pow_zero, one_sub_one_eq_zero, zero_div]

    simp [hi, h1]
