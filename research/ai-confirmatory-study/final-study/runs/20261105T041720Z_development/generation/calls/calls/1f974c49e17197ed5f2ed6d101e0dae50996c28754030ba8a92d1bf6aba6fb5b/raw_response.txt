STATEMENT: For all natural numbers \(a\) and \(b\), if \(a \geq b\) and \(b \geq 1\), then \(a^3 \geq b^3\).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0158 (a b : Nat) (h1 : a ≥ b) (h2 : b ≥ 1) : a^3 ≥ b^3 := by
  induction a with
  | zero =>
    have : 0^3 ≥ b^3 := by
      rw [Nat.zero_pow 3]; 
      linarith [h2]
    exact this
  | succ a' ih =>
    have : a^3 = (succ a')^3 := by
      rw [Nat.succ_mul, Nat.mul_assoc, Nat.pow_add, Nat.pow_one, ih]
    have : (succ a')^3 ≥ b^3 := by
      linarith [h1, this]
    exact this
```
