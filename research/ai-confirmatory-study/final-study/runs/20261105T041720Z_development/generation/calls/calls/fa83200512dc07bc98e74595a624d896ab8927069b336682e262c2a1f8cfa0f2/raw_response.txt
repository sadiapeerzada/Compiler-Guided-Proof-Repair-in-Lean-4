STATEMENT: For any two lists \(a\) and \(b\) of natural numbers, the length of the list obtained by concatenating \(a\) and \(b\) is equal to the sum of the lengths of \(a\) and \(b\).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0153 (a b : List Nat) : (a ++ b).length = a.length + b.length := by
  induction a with
  | nil => simp [List.length, List.append]
  | cons x xs ih =>
    induction b with
    | nil => simp [List.length, List.append]
    | cons y ys =>
      simp [ih, List.length, List.append, Nat.add_assoc]
```
