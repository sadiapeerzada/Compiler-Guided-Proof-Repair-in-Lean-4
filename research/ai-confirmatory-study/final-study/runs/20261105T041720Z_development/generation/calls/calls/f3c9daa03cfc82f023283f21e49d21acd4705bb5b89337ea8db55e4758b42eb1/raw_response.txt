STATEMENT: For any positive integer \( n \) and any real number \( a \) such that \( a \neq 1 \), the sum of the first \( n \) terms of a geometric series with the first term \( a \) and common ratio \( a \) is given by \( \frac{a(1 - a^n)}{1 - a} \).
