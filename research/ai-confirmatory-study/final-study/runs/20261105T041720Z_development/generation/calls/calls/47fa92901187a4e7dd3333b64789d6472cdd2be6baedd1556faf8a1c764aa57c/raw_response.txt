STATEMENT: For all real numbers \(x\) and \(y\), the composition of the sine and cosine functions satisfies the identity \(\sin(\cos(x) + \cos(y)) = \cos(\sin(x) + \sin(y))\).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0061 (x y : ℝ) : sin (cos x + cos y) = cos (sin x + sin y) := by
  have h1 : sin (cos x + cos y) = cos (π/2 - (cos x + cos y)) := by
    rw [sin_sub, cos_add, pi_half_sub]
  have h2 : cos (sin x + sin y) = sin (π/2 - (sin x + sin y)) := by
    rw [cos_sub, sin_add, pi_half_sub]
  have h3 : π/2 - (cos x + cos y) = π/2 - (sin x + sin y) := by
    rw [pi_half_sub, cos_add, sin_add]
  exact h2.trans h3.trans h1
```
