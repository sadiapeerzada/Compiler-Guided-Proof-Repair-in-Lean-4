STATEMENT: If \(A\) and \(B\) are finite sets and \(f: A \to B\) is a function, then the image of the union of any two subsets of \(A\) is a subset of the union of their images under \(f\).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0108 {α β : Type*} {f : α → β} {A B C : Set α} (hA : finite A) (hB : finite B) : 
  f '' (A ∪ B) ⊆ f '' A ∪ f '' B := by
  have hAB : A ∪ B = (A ∪ B) := by simp
  have hAUB : f '' (A ∪ B) = f '' A ∪ f '' B := by
    rw [image_union, hAB]
  exact hAUB
```
