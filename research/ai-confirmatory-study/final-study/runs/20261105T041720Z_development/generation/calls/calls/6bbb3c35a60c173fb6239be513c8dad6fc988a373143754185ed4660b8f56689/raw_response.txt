STATEMENT: For all integers \(a\) and \(b\), the absolute value of their difference is less than or equal to the sum of their absolute values.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0100 (a b : Int) : |a - b| ≤ |a| + |b| := by
  have h1 : |a - b| = |b - a| := by
    simp only [abs_sub, abs_sub]
  have h2 : |a - b| ≤ |a| + |b - a| := by
    simp only [h1, abs_add]
  have h3 : |a| + |b - a| ≤ |a| + |b| := by
    simp only [abs_eq_zero_or_pos, abs_eq_zero_or_pos, le_add_of_nonneg_right, le_add_of_nonneg_left]
  exact h2.trans h3
```
