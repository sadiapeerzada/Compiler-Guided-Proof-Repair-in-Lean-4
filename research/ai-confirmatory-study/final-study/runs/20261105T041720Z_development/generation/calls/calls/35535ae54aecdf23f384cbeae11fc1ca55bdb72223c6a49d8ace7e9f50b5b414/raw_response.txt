STATEMENT: For any lists \(a\), \(b\), and \(c\), the reversal of the concatenation of \(a\) and \(b\) followed by \(c\) is equal to the concatenation of the reversals of \(b\) and \(c\) followed by the reversal of \(a\).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0080 (a b c : List α) : (a ++ b) ++ c = (c.reverse ++ b.reverse).reverse ++ a.reverse := by
  induction a with x xs ih
  · simp [List.reverse_cons, List.append_assoc, List.reverse_append]
  · simp [List.append_assoc, ih, List.reverse_append, List.reverse_cons, List.append_assoc, List.reverse_append]
```
