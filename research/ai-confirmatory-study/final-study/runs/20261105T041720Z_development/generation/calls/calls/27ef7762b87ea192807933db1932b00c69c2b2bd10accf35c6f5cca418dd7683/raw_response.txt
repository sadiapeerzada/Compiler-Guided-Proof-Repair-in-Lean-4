STATEMENT: For any list of natural numbers `l`, the sum of the list obtained by recursively appending the number 1 to the end of `l` is equal to the sum of `l` plus the length of `l`.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0117 (l : List Nat) : List.sum (l ++ [1]) = List.sum l + l.length := by
  induction l with
  | nil => 
    simp [List.append, List.sum, List.length]
  | cons x xs ih =>
    simp [List.append, List.sum, List.length, ih]
```
