STATEMENT: For any finite sets \(A\) and \(B\), and any function \(f: A \times B \to \mathbb{Z}\), the sum over all elements of \(A \times B\) is equal to the sum of the sums over all elements of \(A\) for each fixed \(b \in B\).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0140 {A B : Set ℕ} {f : A × B → ℤ} : 
  (∑ (a, b) in A × B, f (a, b)) = ∑ b, ∑ a, f (a, b) := by
  simp only [Set.prod_sum, Set.sum_comm]
```
