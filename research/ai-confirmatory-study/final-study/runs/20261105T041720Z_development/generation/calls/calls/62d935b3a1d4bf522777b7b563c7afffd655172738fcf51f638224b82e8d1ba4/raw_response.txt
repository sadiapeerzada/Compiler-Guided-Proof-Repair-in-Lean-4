STATEMENT: For any list of natural numbers `l`, the sum of the list obtained by recursively appending the number 1 to the end of `l` is one more than the sum of `l`.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0021 (l : List Nat) : List.sum (l ++ [1]) = List.sum l + 1 := by
  induction l with
  | nil => 
    simp [List.append, List.sum]
  | cons x xs ih => 
    simp [List.append, List.sum, ih]
```
