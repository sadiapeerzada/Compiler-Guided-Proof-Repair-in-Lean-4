STATEMENT: For any two finite lists `l1` and `l2`, the concatenation of `l1` reversed with `l2` reversed is equal to the reverse of the concatenation of `l1` and `l2`.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0200 {α : Type _} (l1 l2 : List α) : (l1.reverse ++ l2.reverse).reverse = (l1 ++ l2).reverse := by
  induction l1 with
  | nil =>
    simp [List.reverse, List.append_nil, List.reverse_append]
  | cons x xs ih =>
    simp [List.reverse, List.append_cons, ih]
```
