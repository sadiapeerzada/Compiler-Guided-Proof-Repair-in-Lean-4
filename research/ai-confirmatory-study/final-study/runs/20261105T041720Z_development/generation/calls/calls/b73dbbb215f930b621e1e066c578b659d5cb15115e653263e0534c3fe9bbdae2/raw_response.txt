STATEMENT: If \(A\) and \(B\) are finite sets and \(f: A \to B\) is a function, then the image of the union of \(A\) and \(B\) under \(f\) is the union of the images of \(A\) and \(B\), i.e., \(f(A \cup B) = f(A) \cup f(B)\).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0084 {α β : Type*} {f : α → β} {A B : Set α} (hA : A.finite) (hB : B.finite) : f '' (A ∪ B) = f '' A ∪ f '' B := by
  ext y
  constructor
  · intro h
    obtain ⟨a, ha⟩ := hA.exists_mem h
    obtain ⟨b, hb⟩ := hB.exists_mem h
    obtain (⟨a, ha⟩ | ⟨b, hb⟩) := classical.em (a ∈ A)
    · exact Union_insert_left ha
    · exact Union_insert_right hb
  · intro ⟨a, ha⟩
    exact Union_insert_left ha
  · intro ⟨b, hb⟩
    exact Union_insert_right hb
```
