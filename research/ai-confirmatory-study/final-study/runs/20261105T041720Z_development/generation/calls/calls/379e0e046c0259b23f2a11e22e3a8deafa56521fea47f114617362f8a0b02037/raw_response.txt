STATEMENT: If \(A\) and \(B\) are finite sets, and \(f: A \to B\) is a function, then the image of the union of two subsets \(X\) and \(Y\) of \(A\) is a subset of the union of the images of \(X\) and \(Y\), i.e., \(f(X \cup Y) \subseteq f(X) \cup f(Y)\).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0228 {α β : Type*} {f : α → β} {X Y : Set α} (hX : X ⊆ α) (hY : Y ⊆ α) : f '' (X ∪ Y) ⊆ f '' X ∪ f '' Y := by
  intro b hb
  obtain ⟨a, haX_or_Y⟩ := Set.mem_union.1 hb
  obtain haX | haY := by
    cases haX_or_Y with a haX haY
    exact Set.mem_image_of_mem _ haX
    exact Set.mem_image_of_mem _ haY
```
