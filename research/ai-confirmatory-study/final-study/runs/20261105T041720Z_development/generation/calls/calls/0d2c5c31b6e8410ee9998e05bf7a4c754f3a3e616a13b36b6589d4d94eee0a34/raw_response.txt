STATEMENT: For any finite list of natural numbers `l`, the sum of the list is equal to the first element of the list plus the sum of the rest of the list.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0189 (l : List Nat) : l.sum = l.head + l.tail.sum := by
  induction l with
  | nil => 
    simp [List.sum_nil, List.head_nil, List.tail_nil]
  | cons h t ih =>
    simp [List.sum_cons, List.head_cons, List.tail_cons, ih]
```
