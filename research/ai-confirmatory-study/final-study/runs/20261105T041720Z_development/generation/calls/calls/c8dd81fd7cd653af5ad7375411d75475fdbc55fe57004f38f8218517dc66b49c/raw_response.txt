STATEMENT: For any lists \(a\), \(b\), and \(c\), the concatenation of \(a\) and the reverse of \(b\) with \(c\) is equal to the reverse of the concatenation of the reverse of \(c\) with \(b\) and then with \(a\).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0152 (a b c : List Nat) : a ++ (b.reverse) = (c.reverse ++ b).reverse ++ a := by
  induction a with x xs ih
  · simp [List.reverse_cons, List.append_assoc, List.reverse_nil]
  · simp [List.reverse_cons, List.append_assoc, ih, List.reverse_append, List.reverse_cons]
```
