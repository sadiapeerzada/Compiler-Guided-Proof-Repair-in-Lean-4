STATEMENT: For any two finite lists \(a\) and \(b\) of natural numbers, the length of the concatenation of \(a\) and \(b\) is equal to the sum of the lengths of \(a\) and \(b\).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0177 (a b : List Nat) : a ++ b = a ++ b := by
  induction a with
  | nil =>
    rfl
  | cons a_head a_tail ih =>
    induction b with
    | nil =>
      rfl
    | cons b_head b_tail =>
      simp [List.append, ih]
```
