STATEMENT: If \(A\) and \(B\) are finite sets and \(f: A \to B\) is a function, then the image of the union of two subsets \(X, Y \subseteq A\) is a subset of the union of their images, i.e., \(f(X \cup Y) \subseteq f(X) \cup f(Y)\).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0156 {A B : Type} [fintype A] [fintype B] {f : A → B} (X Y : set A) : f '' (X ∪ Y) ⊆ f '' X ∪ f '' Y := by
  intro b
  obtain a | a := mem_or_not_mem b (f '' (X ∪ Y))
  · obtain ha | ha := mem_or_not_mem b (f '' X)
    · obtain hX | hX := mem_or_not_mem a X
      · exact or.inl (mem_image_of_mem _ hX)
      · exact or.inr (mem_image_of_mem _ hX)
    · exact or.inl (mem_image_of_mem _ ha)
  · obtain hY | hY := mem_or_not_mem a Y
    · exact or.inl (mem_image_of_mem _ hY)
    · exact or.inr (mem_image_of_mem _ hY)
```
