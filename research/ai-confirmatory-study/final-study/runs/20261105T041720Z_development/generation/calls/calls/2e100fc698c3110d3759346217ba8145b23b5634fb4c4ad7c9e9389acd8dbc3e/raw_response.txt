STATEMENT: For any list of natural numbers `l`, the sum of the list obtained by appending the number 0 to the end of `l` is equal to the sum of `l` plus 0.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0165 (l : List Nat) : List.sum (l ++ [0]) = List.sum l + 0 := by
  simp [List.append, List.sum]
```
