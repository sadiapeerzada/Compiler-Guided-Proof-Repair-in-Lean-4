STATEMENT: For any lists \(a\) and \(b\), the concatenation of \(a\) and the reversal of \(b\) is equal to the reversal of the concatenation of the reversal of \(a\) and \(b\).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0224 (a b : List Nat) : a ++ (b.reverse) = (a.reverse ++ b).reverse := by
  induction a with
  | nil => simp [List.reverse]
  | cons a_head a_tail ih =>
    simp [List.reverse, ih, List.append_assoc, List.append_reverse, List.append]
```
