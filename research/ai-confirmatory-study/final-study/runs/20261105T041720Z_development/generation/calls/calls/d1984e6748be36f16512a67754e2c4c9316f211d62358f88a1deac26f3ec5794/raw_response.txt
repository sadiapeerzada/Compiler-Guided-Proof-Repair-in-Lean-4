STATEMENT: For any list of natural numbers `l`, if the length of `l` is greater than or equal to 2, then the length of the list after removing the first element is less than the original length.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0010 (l : List Nat) (h : l.length ≥ 2) : (l.tail).length < l.length := by
  have hl1 : l.length > 1 := by
    exact h.nat_cast
  have hl2 : (l.tail).length = l.length - 1 := by
    simp [List.length_tail]
  exact hl2.trans (by linarith)
```
