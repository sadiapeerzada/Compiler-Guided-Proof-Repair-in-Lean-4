STATEMENT: For any natural number \( n \) and any real number \( a \), the sum of the first \( n \) terms of a geometric series with the first term \( a \) and common ratio \( 1 \) is equal to \( n \cdot a \).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0151 (n : Nat) (a : Real) : ∑ i in Finset.range n, a = n * a := by
  induction n with
  | zero => simp [Finset.range_zero]
  | succ n ih => 
    simp [Finset.range_succ, add_comm, ih, mul_add]
```
