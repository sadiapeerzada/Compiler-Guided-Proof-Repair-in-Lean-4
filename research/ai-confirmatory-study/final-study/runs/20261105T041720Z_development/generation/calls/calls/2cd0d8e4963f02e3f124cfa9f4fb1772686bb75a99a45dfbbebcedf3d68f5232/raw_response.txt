STATEMENT: For any finite set \(A\) and any finite sequence of elements \(x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n\) in \(A\), the product of the sequence is equal to the product of the sequence with the elements reindexed by any bijection \(f\) from \(\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}\) to \(\{1, 2, \ldots, n\}\).
