STATEMENT: For any natural number \( n \) and any real number \( a \), the sum of the first \( n \) terms of a geometric series with the first term \( a \) and common ratio \( 2 \) is given by \( S_n = a \cdot (2^n - 1) \).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0175 (n : Nat) (a : Real) : a * (2^n - 1) = ∑ i in Finset.range n, a * (2 ^ i) := by
  induction n with
  | zero =>
    simp [Finset.range_zero, sum_empty, zero_sub]
  | succ n ih =>
    have : Finset.range (succ n) = Finset.insert (succ (Finset.choose (succ n))) (Finset.range n) := by
      simp [Finset.range_succ, Finset.choose_succ]
    rw [this]
    simp [sum_insert, ih, pow_add, one_mul]
```
