STATEMENT: For any integers \(a\) and \(b\), the absolute value of their difference is less than or equal to the sum of their absolute values.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0220 (a b : Int) : |a - b| ≤ |a| + |b| := by
  have h1 : |a - b| = |b - a| := by simp [Int.abs_sub_symm]
  have h2 : |a| + |b| ≥ 0 := by norm_num
  have h3 : |a - b| ≥ 0 := by norm_num
  have h4 : |a - b| ≤ |a| + |b - a| := by
    have : a - b = a + (-b) := by simp
    · rw [this, Int.abs_add, Int.abs_nonneg, Int.abs_nonneg]
      apply le_add_right
  have h5 : |a| + |b - a| ≤ |a| + |b| := by
    have : |b - a| = |a - b| := by simp [Int.abs_sub_symm]
    · rw [this, h4]
      apply le_add_left
  exact h5
```
