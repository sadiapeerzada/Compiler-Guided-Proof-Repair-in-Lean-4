STATEMENT: For any finite sequence of arithmetic progressions \(a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n\) with common differences \(d_1, d_2, \ldots, d_n\) and first terms \(a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n\) respectively, the sum of the sums of these arithmetic progressions is equal to the sum of the sums of the individual terms of each progression.
