STATEMENT: If \(A\) and \(B\) are finite sets and \(f: A \to B\) is a function, then the image of the union of any two subsets of \(A\) is a subset of the union of their images. That is, for any \(X, Y \subseteq A\), \(f(X \cup Y) \subseteq f(X) \cup f(Y)\).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0060 {α β : Type*} {f : α → β} {X Y : Set α} (hX : X ⊆ A) (hY : Y ⊆ A) : f (X ∪ Y) ⊆ f X ∪ f Y := by
  intro b hb
  obtain ⟨a, ha⟩ := hb
  obtain haX : a ∈ X := by
    exact hX a ha
  obtain haY : a ∈ Y := by
    exact hY a ha
  exact Or.elim (Or.inl haX) (Or.inr haY)
```
