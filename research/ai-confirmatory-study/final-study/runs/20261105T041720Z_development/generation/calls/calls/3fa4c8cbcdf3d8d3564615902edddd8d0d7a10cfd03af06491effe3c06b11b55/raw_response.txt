STATEMENT: For any list of natural numbers `l`, the sum of the list obtained by appending a zero to the end of `l` is equal to the sum of `l` plus zero.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0069 (l : List Nat) : List.sum (l ++ [0]) = List.sum l + 0 := by
  induction l with
  | nil => 
    simp [List.sum]
  | cons x xs ih => 
    simp [List.sum, ih]
```
