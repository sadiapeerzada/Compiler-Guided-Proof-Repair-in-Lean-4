STATEMENT: For any natural numbers \(a\), \(b\), \(c\), and \(d\), if \(a\) divides \(b\) and \(c\) divides \(d\), then \(ac\) divides \(bd\).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0173 (a b c d : Nat) (h1 : a ∣ b) (h2 : c ∣ d) : a * c ∣ b * d := by
  obtain ⟨k, hk1⟩ := h1
  obtain ⟨l, hk2⟩ := h2
  have : b * d = (k * b) * (l * d) := by
    rw [mul_assoc, hk1, hk2]
  rw [this]
  exact mul_div_mul_left (k * l) b d
```
