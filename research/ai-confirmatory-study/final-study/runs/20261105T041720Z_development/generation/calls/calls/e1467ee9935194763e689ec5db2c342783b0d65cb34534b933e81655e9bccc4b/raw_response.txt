STATEMENT: For any integer \( n \), if \( n^2 \) is divisible by 4, then \( n \) is even.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0231 (n : Int) (h : 4 ∣ n^2) : 2 ∣ n := by
  have h1 : ∃ k, n^2 = 4 * k := by
    rw [h] at h ; exact h
  obtain ⟨k, hk⟩ := h1
  have h2 : n * n = 4 * k := by
    rw [hk]
  have h3 : n * n = 2 * (2 * k) := by
    rw [h2]
  have h4 : 2 ∣ (n * n) := by
    rw [h3] ; exact Int.mul_divisible_right 2 (2 * k)
  exact h4
```
