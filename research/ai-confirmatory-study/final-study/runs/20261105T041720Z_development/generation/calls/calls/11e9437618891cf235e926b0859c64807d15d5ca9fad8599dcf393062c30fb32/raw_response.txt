STATEMENT: For any natural number \( n \) and any real number \( a \), the sum of the first \( n \) terms of a geometric series with the first term \( a \) and common ratio \( 1 \) is equal to \( n \cdot a \).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0223 (n : Nat) (a : Real) : ∑ i in Finset.range n, a = n * a := by
  induction n with
  | zero => simp [Finset.range_zero]
  | succ n ih =>
    have : ∑ i in Finset.range (succ n), a = (Finset.range (succ n)).toList.sum a := by
      simp [Finset.range_succ, List.sum_eq_fold, ih]
    rw [Finset.range_succ, List.toList_sum, this, succ_mul]
```
