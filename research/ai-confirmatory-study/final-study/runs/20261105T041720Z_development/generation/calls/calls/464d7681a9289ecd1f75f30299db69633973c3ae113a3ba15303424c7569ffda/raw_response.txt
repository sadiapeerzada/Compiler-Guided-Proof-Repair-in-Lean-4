STATEMENT: For all real numbers \(x\) and \(y\), the composition of the sine and cosine functions satisfies the identity \(\sin(\cos(x) + \cos(y)) = \sin(\cos(x))\cos(\sin(y)) + \cos(\sin(x))\sin(\cos(y))\).

LEAN:
```lean

  have h1 : cos x + cos y = cos x + cos y := by
    simp
  have h2 : sin (cos x + cos y) = sin (cos x + cos y) := by
    simp
  have h3 : sin (cos x + cos y) = sin (cos x) * cos (sin y) + cos (sin x) * sin (cos y) := by
    rw [sin_add, cos_add, sin_cos_add, cos_sin_add, sin_squared_add_cos_squared]
    ring
```
