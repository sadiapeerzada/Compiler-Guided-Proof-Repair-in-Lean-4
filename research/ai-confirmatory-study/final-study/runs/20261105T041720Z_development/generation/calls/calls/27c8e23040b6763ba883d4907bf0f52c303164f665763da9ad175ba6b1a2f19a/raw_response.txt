STATEMENT: If \(A\) and \(B\) are finite sets, and \(f: A \to B\) is a function, then the image of the union of any two subsets of \(A\) is a subset of the union of their images. That is, for any \(X, Y \subseteq A\), \(f(X \cup Y) \subseteq f(X) \cup f(Y)\).

LEAN:
```lean
import Mathlib.Algebra.GroupWithOne.Basic
import Mathlib.Data.Finset.Basic

theorem final_dev_0012 {α β : Type*} [fintype α] [fintype β] {f : α → β} {X Y : Finset α} : f '' (X ∪ Y) ⊆ f '' X ∪ f '' Y := by
  intro b hb
  obtain ⟨a, ha⟩ := Finset.mem_image.1 hb
  obtain ⟨hx, hy⟩ := ha
  left
  · exact f a
    · exact hx
    · exact Finset.mem_image_of_mem X a hx
    · exact Finset.mem_image_of_mem Y a hy
```
