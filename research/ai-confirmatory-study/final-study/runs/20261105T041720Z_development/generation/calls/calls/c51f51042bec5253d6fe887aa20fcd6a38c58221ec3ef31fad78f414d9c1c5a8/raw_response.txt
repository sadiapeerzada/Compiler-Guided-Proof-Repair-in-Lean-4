STATEMENT: For all integers \(a\) and \(b\), the absolute value of the difference between \(a\) and \(b\) is less than or equal to the sum of their absolute values.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0148 (a b : Int) : |a - b| ≤ |a| + |b| := by
  have h1 : |a - b| = |b - a| := by
    simp [Int.abs_sub_symm]
  have h2 : |a| + |b| ≥ 0 := by
    norm_num
  have h3 : |a - b| + |b - a| ≤ |a| + |b| + |a - b| := by
    simp [h1]
  have h4 : |a - b| ≤ |a| + |b| := by
    linarith [h3, h2]
  exact h4
```
