STATEMENT: If \(A\) and \(B\) are finite sets and \(f: A \to B\) is a function, then the image of the union of two subsets \(X, Y \subseteq A\) is a subset of the union of their images, i.e., \(f(X \cup Y) \subseteq f(X) \cup f(Y)\).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0132 {A B : Type*} [fintype A] [fintype B] {f : A → B} (X Y : set A) : f '' (X ∪ Y) ⊆ f '' X ∪ f '' Y := by
  intro b
  obtain h : ∃ a, a ∈ X ∪ Y ∧ f a = b := by
    use some a
    cases a with a ha
    exact ⟨a, ha, by simp⟩
  obtain (hX : a ∈ X) | (hY : a ∈ Y) := h.2
  · obtain hX' : f '' X ∩ {b} ≠ ∅ := by
      exact ⟨⟨_, hX⟩, by simp⟩
    obtain hX'' : f '' X ∩ {b} = {b} := by
      exact hX'
    exact hX''.1
  · obtain hY' : f '' Y ∩ {b} ≠ ∅ := by
      exact ⟨⟨_, hY⟩, by simp⟩
    obtain hY'' : f '' Y ∩ {b} = {b} := by
      exact hY'
    exact hY''.1
```
