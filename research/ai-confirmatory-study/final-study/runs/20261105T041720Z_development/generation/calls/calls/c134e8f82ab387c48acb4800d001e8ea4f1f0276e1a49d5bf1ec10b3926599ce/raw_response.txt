STATEMENT: For any list of natural numbers `l`, if the length of `l` is at least 2, then the length of the list obtained by removing the first element and the last element of `l` is at least 0.

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0130 (l : List Nat) (h : l.length ≥ 2) : (l.drop 1).drop (l.length - 2).length ≥ 0 := by
  have hl1 : l.length > 1 := by
    from h
    exact Nat.lt_of_le_of_ne_of_gt h (Nat.ne_of_gt h)
  have hl2 : (l.drop 1).length = l.length - 1 := by
    rw [List.drop_length, hl1]
  have hl3 : (l.drop 1).drop (l.length - 2).length = (l.length - 1) - (l.length - 2) := by
    rw [hl2, List.drop_length]
  have hl4 : (l.length - 1) - (l.length - 2) = 1 := by
    ring
  exact Nat.zero_le hl4
```
