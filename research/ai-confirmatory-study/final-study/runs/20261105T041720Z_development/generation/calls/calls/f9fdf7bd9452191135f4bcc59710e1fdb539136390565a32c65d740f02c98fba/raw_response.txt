STATEMENT: For any finite sequence of arithmetic progressions \(a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n\) with common differences \(d_1, d_2, \ldots, d_n\) and first terms \(a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n\) respectively, the sum of the sums of these progressions is equal to the sum of the first terms plus the sum of the common differences times the number of progressions minus one.
