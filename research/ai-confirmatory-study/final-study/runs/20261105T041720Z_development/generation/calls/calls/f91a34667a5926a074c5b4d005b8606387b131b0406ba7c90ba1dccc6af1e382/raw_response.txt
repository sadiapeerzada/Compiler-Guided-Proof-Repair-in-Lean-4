STATEMENT: For any finite sets \(A\) and \(B\), the cardinality of the set difference \(A \setminus B\) is equal to the cardinality of \(A\) minus the cardinality of the intersection of \(A\) and \(B\).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0131 {α : Type _} {A B : Set α} (h : Finite A) (hB : Finite B) : 
  card (A \ B) = card A - card (A ∩ B) := by
  have hAB : Finite (A ∩ B) := by
    rw [Finite.inter]
    exact hB
  have hABc : Finite (A \ B) := by
    rw [Finite.diff]
    exact h
    exact hAB
  have hABc_card : card (A \ B) = card A - card (A ∩ B) := by
    rw [Finite.card_diff h hAB]
  exact hABc_card
```
