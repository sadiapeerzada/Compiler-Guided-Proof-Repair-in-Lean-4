STATEMENT: For any finite sequence of arithmetic progressions \(a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n\) with common differences \(d_1, d_2, \ldots, d_n\) and initial terms \(a_1, a_2, \ldots, a_n\) respectively, the sum of the sums of these arithmetic progressions is equal to the sum of the initial terms plus the sum of the common differences times the number of terms minus one, divided by two.
