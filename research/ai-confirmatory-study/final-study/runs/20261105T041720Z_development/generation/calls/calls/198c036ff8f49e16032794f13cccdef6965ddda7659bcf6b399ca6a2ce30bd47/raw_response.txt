STATEMENT: For any lists \(a\) and \(b\), the concatenation of \(a\) reversed with \(b\) reversed is the reverse of the concatenation of \(b\) with \(a\).

LEAN:
```lean
theorem final_dev_0104 (a b : List Nat) : (a.reverse ++ b.reverse).reverse = (b ++ a).reverse := by
  induction a with
  | nil =>
    rfl
  | cons a_head a_tail ih =>
    simp [List.reverse, List.append, ih]
```
