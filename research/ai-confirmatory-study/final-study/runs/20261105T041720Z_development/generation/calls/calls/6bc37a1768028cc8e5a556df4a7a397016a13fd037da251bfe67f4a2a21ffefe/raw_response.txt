STATEMENT: For any integers \(a\), \(b\), and \(c\), if \(d = \gcd(a, b)\), then there exist integers \(x\) and \(y\) such that \(d = ax + by\).

theorem final_dev_0016 (a b : ℤ) (d : ℤ) (h : d = gcd a b) : ∃ x y : ℤ, d = a*x + b*y := by
