# Study 2: Controlled Lean 4 Proof Repair

Separate constructed-theorem experiment, not a replay of
the original frozen 60-theorem study.

Development: 12 theorems, one model, one seed.
Test: 24 theorems, three seeds (2027, 2028, 2029).

Test verification outcomes:
- Initial: 4/72
- Neutral retry: 5/72
- Compiler-guided: 10/72
- Shuffled diagnostics: 9/72
- Token-matched: 5/72
- Independent resampling: 5/72

Observed differences are statistically inconclusive.
Correct diagnostics did not clearly outperform shuffled diagnostics.

The published tables are processed experimental evidence,
not a complete raw-inference replay archive.
Original study and publication artifacts are unchanged.
