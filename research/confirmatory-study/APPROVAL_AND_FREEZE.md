# Human decisions and release gate

Current state: **NO_GO** for development and test. No independent reviewer, fresh approved subject or model-inference authorization has been supplied. The target240 test/40 development subjects are not obtained data. The empty candidate arrays and header-only results are deliberate; historical items cannot be recycled into this new confirmatory test.

The gate checks artifacts and exact hashes; it cannot establish a person's identity, independence, signature authenticity or legal authority. A software GO is therefore a consistency result for already supplied human authorization, never its replacement. Human decision fields must be completed by the actual responsible people, not Codex.

## Required handoff, in order

1. **Independent curator:** collect genuinely new source/licensed theorems and NL specifications, verified reference proofs, family labels and unique opaque IDs. Keep test statements from protocol developers/calibration. Run `dataset_tools.py CANDIDATES NEW_DIRECTORY` to screen historical statement/candidate/family overlap and within-cohort alpha duplicates. All flagged items are quarantined; a clean lexical screen is not mathematical approval. Whole-family deterministic partitioning preserves group boundaries; excess/oversize groups stay reserved and actual sample counts must be reported.
2. **Curator/Lean expert:** use `reference_check.py CANDIDATES NEW_PROOF_DIRECTORY --curator-reference-validation` offline to check exact references under the proposed import/verifier policy. Add each bound proof artifact to its row as `reference_verification:{path,sha256}`. Rebuild the final manifest after adding these fields. Library/source and mathematical/NL judgments still require independent review.
3. **Independent mathematics reviewer:** sign `APPROVED_BENCHMARK` with `actor_kind:independent_human`, name, independence statement, signed date and evidence artifact path/hash. Bind the exact dataset-manifest SHA and every subject hash. Per-subject decisions must cover mathematics, semantic fidelity, family independence, licensing and historical-overlap clearance. The existing historical human template remains pending, not a shortcut to fresh-test approval.
4. **Research lead/statistical reviewer:** approve actual sample size/precision, source/model/environment pins, exact padding/donor/order/analysis rules and missingness. Produce a new approved protocol/version with status `FROZEN_FOR_EXECUTION` and signed `FROZEN_BEFORE_OUTCOMES`, binding protocol, data manifest, source lock and benchmark-approval hashes. Attest that neither test statements nor test outcomes were used for calibration/tuning; independent benchmark review is a separate curator activity. Freeze before test inference. Engineering locks are not preregistration or human approval.
5. **Compute authority:** provide `AUTHORIZED_EXECUTION`, `actor_kind:human_authority` (or independent human where appropriate), real name/authority statement, signed date/evidence, selected phase, model revision, protocol/data/code/benchmark/freeze/verification-evidence hashes, and explicit call/runtime caps. A research author may authorize resources without pretending to be an independent mathematical reviewer.
6. **Operator:** supply a local immutable snapshot with `CONFIRMATORY_MODEL_SNAPSHOT`, verify package versions/actual Lean and clean pinned Mathlib, then use the selected phase's gate. Default command is status, not execution. No phase may use another phase's authorization.

Public partition manifests expose counts, subject hashes, statement fingerprints and family labels, not test statement text. Calibration loads only development rows; tests guard against opening the sealed test file. External curators must also maintain role separation and access logs; software cannot prove nobody inspected a theorem elsewhere.

## Integrity and execution semantics

Equal input length and output cap do not guarantee equal realized output tokens, latency, semantic irrelevance of donor feedback or every causal mechanism.

Initial successes are not repaired; missing repair comparisons stay in bounds among known initial failures. Raw strict outcomes remain separate from invalid experimental endpoints. Reference proofs are never sent to the model. Model output is extracted only from the durable response, never replaced with a reference proof.
